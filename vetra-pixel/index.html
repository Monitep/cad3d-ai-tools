Italiano

Vetra ricorda le ultime impostazioni di elaborazione ed esportazione. Salva e richiama le tue configurazioni preferite per nome. Puoi aprire immagini AVIF ed esportare in AVIF con qualità regolabile e trasparenza.

English

Vetra remembers your last processing and export settings. Save and recall your favorite configurations by name. Open AVIF images and export AVIF with adjustable quality and transparency.

Local image restoration and upscaling. Requires Windows 10 22H2 64-bit or Windows 11 64-bit. Run the installer: it detects the previous installation, automatically closes Vetra instances in the folder being updated, and keeps models and settings. Finish running jobs before installing. If an old ZIP copy cannot be located reliably, select its folder once. Models download on demand.
