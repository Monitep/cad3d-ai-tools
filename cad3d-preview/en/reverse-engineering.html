Reverse engineering

From the object.
To the digital model.

Rebuild the geometry of an existing element to develop, modify or visualise it. The starting point is the object, together with available measurements and references.

Physical and digital.
The shape is rebuilt.

Photograph and model of the same component from the CAD3D.Expert portfolio. The visual presentation of both is enhanced with AI.

A process defined
around your component.

01

Gather references

The object, measurements, photographs, drawings or existing scans. We agree on scale, areas to rebuild and required detail.

02

Rebuild the geometry

Surfaces and volumes take shape in the CAD model. We share the steps needed to review proportions and the interpretation of references.

03

Prepare the model

We agree on formats, intended use and required tolerances. Dimensional checks and manufacturing requirements are defined in the brief.
